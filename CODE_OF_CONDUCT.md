# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

Everyone participating in this project is expected to communicate with respect, patience, and professionalism.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving helpful feedback
- Accepting different experience levels
- Asking questions clearly
- Helping others learn

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Insulting or offensive comments
- Discrimination of any kind
- Trolling or disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block participants who violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainer.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and privately as possible.
